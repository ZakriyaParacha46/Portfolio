// about

Curious about how things work, down to the transistors

I'm a Software Engineer at Motive with a Computer Engineering background (BE, NUST–CEME, 2024) and hands-on experience across embedded systems, FPGA development, and robotics. That curiosity goes back to childhood tinkering with electronics, and it's shaped years of projects spanning FPGA logic design up through backend web services.

My core interests are embedded systems, IoT, FPGA/ASIC design, and computer architecture, and increasingly, software architecture and system design. I'm currently building professional experience in backend engineering while working toward a long-term move back into embedded and hardware-focused roles.
